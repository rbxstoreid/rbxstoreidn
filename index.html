OUR STORY

The Story Behind RBXSTORE.ID

RBXSTORE.ID began on July 30, 2025, from a simple idea that came to its founder during a school day. What started as a small thought about selling online gaming products soon developed into something more serious.

In its earliest days, RBXSTORE.ID did not have a website or a digital storefront. The first product menu was organised using paper while the store was still being developed and tested.

As the idea continued to grow, a friend named Jesslyn helped develop the store by creating an improved product menu using Canva. This gave RBXSTORE.ID a more organised way to present its products to customers.

Over time, the store continued to improve its catalogue, introduced promotions and discounts, and expanded the selection of gaming products available to customers.

In July 2026, RBXSTORE.ID reached another important milestone when its founder began developing an independent website for the store. The project evolved from a paper-based menu into a digital storefront built using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GitHub and GitHub Pages.

Today, RBXSTORE.ID continues to operate and develop with the goal of providing a simple, convenient and organised way for customers to purchase digital gaming products.

Established July 30, 2025.
